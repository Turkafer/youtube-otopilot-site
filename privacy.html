Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

YouTube Otopilot ("the App") is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel ("we"). It is used only to manage that channel.

Data we access

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the App accesses through the YouTube API Services:

  • The channel's videos, playlists and their metadata (to upload and organise videos)
  • Public comments on the channel's videos (to reply to them)
  • The channel's YouTube Analytics data (to create performance reports for the owner)

How we use and store data

  • Data is used only to operate the channel and produce reports for the channel owner.
  • OAuth tokens, reports and a small activity database are stored locally on the owner's own computer or server.
  • Comment text may be sent to an AI service provider solely to draft a reply. It is not used for any other purpose.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with the channel you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The channel owner can revoke the App's access at any time at Google security settings.

Data deletion

Locally stored data is deleted automatically after it is no longer needed, or immediately on request. To request deletion of data related to you, contact us at the address below.
